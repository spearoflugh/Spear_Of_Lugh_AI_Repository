# Facts by Fiat

CDC has, once again, changed their guidelines with relation to the COVID19. A brief report is the following Twitter thread:

[Embedded X post]

My point here is not to comment on the medical accuracy of recommandations during this epidemics but rather how *truth* falls from the sky (here the CDC) in our social media driven society. This is what I call the social Oracle problem. It is a distant cousin of the legit blockchain oracle problem. The latter being that when you have smart contract triggered by real life event there is no clear way to make the blockchain aware that the event took place. It is a serious issue because if you start to talk about decentralized finance and that the first thing you do is to centralize again at the first use ... why bother? The former appears to be the natural child of the old institutions and the social media era.

### Follow the silence

Sometimes we should do more like bats. Bats use ultrasound to track their preys. When there is no echo it is also informative for them: it means that there is nothing in front of them and that they are not going to crash into a wall or a tree. What is not said is often as interesting as what is explicitely stated. In the case of the CDC guidance, the fact that the guidances contradict each other in a matter weeks for instance. It is even more disturbing than wrong advices: one can recall that at the start of the pandemics you were strongly discouraged from going to the beach etc. The issue here is the lack of accountability. Remind that, following those guidelines, many lives have been crushed (people were refused to work, have a social life, you name it). There is not a word about that on the CDC press release. Past performance is not indicative of future results...or not.

This is a tripartite game: the political leaders, the scientific institutions and the vox populi, or rather the social media aka the swarm. The political leaders will say they follow the science, the scientific institutions say they are the science and the swarm is supposed to keep them in check (fact checkers, debunkers of the world if you hear me). No one does what is claimed to be done. Political leaders no longer take decisions, scientific institutions don't learn from experience and the swarm as a 3 days long accountability frame, past those 72 hours everything has fallen in the dark. Every participant is too narrowly focused on the present, say stupid things about the future and never think about what has happened to learn lessons from our mistakes.

[Image caption: Burning bushes]

### Falling from the sky

This is through this trinity 4.0 that *truth* comes to the public sphere today. Instead of following the dialectic, journalistic and scientific processes, talking points are *revealed* to the pleb as a divine message: You don't talk about it - you don't emit any doubt on how it was formed - you have to shut up, to believe and then to obey. If we follow some guidelines during this pandemics it gives something like: Don't go outside - Masks are useless - You can go outside and you should open the windows - Masks are mandatory - The vaccines are safe and effective - this is a pandemics of the unvaccinated - actually you are going to make midler versions of the covid if boosted... The down spirale is without ending in sight. At each step the truth of the moment aka the current thing:

[Embedded X post]

It eclispes everything including the old truths, or the one presented as such. So much for the timelessness of truth. What is troubling is how easily everything has been accepted. Very strong limitations on civil liberties were adopted. I could have never imagined having to show a qr-code to go to a restaurant before the COVID19 crisis. I could have never imagined that *exceptional and temporary circumstances* could span over two years. Yet it is because the situation was supposedly "so dire" that everything had been redefined. From the word vaccine to the vote of laws to exclude responsability of pharmaceutical companies with relation to vaccines etc. The questions that people were talking about were more about "how far should we go ?" than "is it really what we are told it is ?".

If you think about it, it is very similar to your social media feed. You are supposed to control it (by following or not some members/subjects) but actually it is completely built in an opaque way - and lets be clear as anyone surely you (myself included) don't give a fuck about that. Of course on Twitter you can check by hand every page of the people you follow. In reality 99% of the times you will go through trends and the automatically generated feed. This is pushed to the extreme on TikTok. Passivity of the spectator is back with a vengeance. It was thought that on the internet people were active contrary to television. It had been true until the early 2010s. Then, there was too much information available and it became impossible to navigate the web without help. Soon the help evolved into suggestions that turned into mandates before anyone knew it. Critical thinking is in more critical conditions than many.  A major factor is the speed at which information circulates. Between the moment Moses witnessed the burning bush and the one where it was discussed the test of time had don its job. Of course looking for facts in religious text is stupid. Religious texts are not here to tell you what is but rather to tell you what to do. Only time can tell if they are wise or not. Today the question is no longer asked because we have forgotten the predicament before the sun is down. We have clearly overpassed the information speed limit. We need to slow it down voluntarily. How could social media been tinkered to help that will be paramount.
