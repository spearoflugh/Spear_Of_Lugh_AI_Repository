# Thematic Map — The Spear of Lugh (v15)

## Central Thesis

The transition from **written culture** to **digital culture** is at least as significant as the
transition from oral to written culture. It changes:
1. The mathematical structure of social space (Topological Revolution — sol-002)
2. The ergodic properties of information (sol-001)
3. The fitness of every institution built for the written era (sol-004, sol-021)
4. The structure of mental/semantic space itself (Second Topological Revolution — sol-024h)

Everything else in the corpus is a consequence, application, or implication of this thesis.
The pre-foundational series (sol-000b through sol-000q) establishes the epistemological
and philosophical preconditions. The numbered series (sol-001 onward) develops the thesis
in chronological order of composition.

---

## Epistemological Foundation — The 2020 Triad (sol-000o / sol-000p / sol-000q)

Before all clusters, three 2020 articles establish the epistemological charter of the corpus:

- **sol-000o (LEG):** Gaps in any system of thought are unavoidable (Godel, Halting Problem).
  The correct response is humility and effort, not concealment. Skepticism applies to itself.
- **sol-000q (Data Diet):** In a world of data overabundance, radical abstinence (epistemic
  anorexia / Anapliroforia) is as dangerous as gluttony. Labelling is the mechanism of abstinence.
- **sol-000p (Lightning Rod):** Attention is controlled not by imposing thoughts but by
  preventing thoughts about what matters. The Huxley model, not the Orwell model.

**Arc:** sol-000o (gaps unavoidable) -> sol-000q (abstinence as response) -> sol-000p
(abstinence exploited) -> all subsequent epistemological and narrative analysis.

This triad is the earliest layer of the corpus (April-July 2020) and the logical prerequisite
for understanding why the diagnosis matters: knowledge is structurally limited, and those
limits are systematically exploited. Every article in Cluster 8 (Narrative Control) is an
application of the principle established here.
