# Thematic Map — The Spear of Lugh

## Central Thesis

The transition from **written culture** to **digital culture** is at least as significant as the
transition from oral to written culture. It changes:
1. The mathematical structure of social space (Topological Revolution — sol-002)
2. The ergodic properties of information (sol-001)
3. The fitness of every institution built for the written era (sol-004, sol-021)

Everything else in the corpus is a consequence, application, or implication of this thesis.

## Relationship to The Fibralist Corner

The two repositories form a complete intellectual project:

- **The Spear of Lugh** = diagnostic corpus. Documents what is breaking down and why.
  Tone: analytical, urgent, sometimes sardonic. Written in the present tense of events.

- **The Fibralist Corner** = constructive corpus. Proposes what should replace it.
  Tone: systematic, architectural, long-term. Written in the conditional of possibility.

Reading both together is required for the full picture.
The Spear of Lugh without The Fibralist Corner is diagnosis without remedy.
The Fibralist Corner without The Spear of Lugh is prescription without evidence.

## Cluster 6 — Space and Strategic Resolution
*How orbital technology resolves the Words/Swords disharmony*

| File | Title | Key Contribution |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| sol-025 | Pale Glider | Weber definition dissolving — Technopopulism |
| sol-026 | The Final Frontier | Orbital topology = uniform force projection |
| sol-027 | Orbital Strike Systems | Technical feasibility — already achievable |

**Core insight:** The Topological Revolution (sol-002) created a fatal disharmony —
information moves at light speed, force moves at horse speed. Orbital weapons systems
resolve this by making the topology of force projection as uniform as the topology
of information. This is the theoretical completion of the corpus's geopolitical argument.

| File | Title | Key Contribution |

| sol-028 | News from the Front Lines | Front line as industrial artifact — now dissolving |

**Core insight:** The front line was an industrial-era invention. Digital culture dissolves it.

distributed market incentive architecture (catalaxia) can replace hierarchical military command
for certain functions — the first real-world prototype of Technopraxis applied to defense.
