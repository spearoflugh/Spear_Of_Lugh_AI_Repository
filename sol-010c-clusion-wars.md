    <concept name="The Six Clusions — Taxonomy of Digital Exclusion Mechanisms">
      The article taxonomizes six mechanisms by which digital culture manages inclusion
      and exclusion, using the war in Ukraine (March 2022) as empirical laboratory.
      Each "clusion" names a distinct operation:

      (1) COLLUSION: instantaneous horizontal coordination at scale. In hours, nation-states,
      corporations, and individuals aligned on Russia=evil without deliberation. This is the
      swarm (sol-000f) operating at geopolitical speed — "before sanctions were voted or
      even decided." The speed itself is the mechanism: there is no longer time for
      Westphalian deliberation.

      (2) INCLUSION (authoritarianism): the new form of control that has no concept of
      an outside. Unlike exclusion authoritarianism (Fascism: exclude the impure by
      deportation/death), inclusion authoritarianism includes everyone under relentless,
      ever-changing compliance conditions. COVID nudges are the prototype. The Ukraine
      sanctions are its geopolitical version: every country must comply. "You are going
      to be fined and bullied to submission."

      (3) EXCLUSION: deplatforming as modern sanction. Financial networks (SWIFT),
      social platforms, airlines, sports bodies — every network layer can be weaponized
      for exclusion. Prefigures sol-049 (HAP, dilution camp) and the ABC logic of
      sol-000j: the sanction is not imprisonment but progressive network disconnection.

      (4) PRECLUSION: preventive language control. The war on vocabulary (banning "war"
      and "invasion" from UN communications in favor of "military offensive") as
      prophylactic exclusion — preventing thoughts before they form. Directly prefigures
      sol-013b (Denycracy) and sol-040 (Discombobulator). The mechanism: talking points
      disseminated by political leaders → taken as ground truth by MSM → spread by
      social media. Framing is prevention.

      (5) OCCLUSION: attentional monopoly of the current thing. Network effects produce
      winner-takes-all in attention: one subject (Ukraine, COVID) takes all bandwidth.
      Hyper-focus is shallow, present-tense only, with no mid- or long-term thinking.
      "Only the instant and the local problem are relevant." Directly prefigures sol-039
      (Hyperpresent and Omnicause) and sol-043 (Babel Timeline).

      (6) SECLUSION: total network disconnection as geopolitical weapon. Russia "secluded"
      from Western networks. But with India and China not following: "the question of who
      is secluded from who starts to bear meaning." Walls closing one brick at a time —
      the gradual, non-punctual nature of digital exclusion distinguishes it from
      physical exclusion (the camp gate closes once; the network disconnects incrementally).
    </concept>
    <concept name="Inclusion Authoritarianism — First Full Articulation">
      This article contains the first complete articulation of inclusion authoritarianism
      in the corpus, predating sol-011c (Great Conflaxion, August 2023) by 17 months.
      The formal distinction: exclusion authoritarianism is punctual and deals with the
      issue once (the Final Solution is explicit); inclusion authoritarianism is relentless

      authoritarianism is the co-exclusion-authoritarianism. Same objects (population
      control, compliance enforcement), morphisms reversed (toward rather than away from
      the system). It is harder to resist precisely because it never offers a clear moment
      of confrontation — the walls close one brick at a time.
    </concept>

<content>
Written three weeks into the Russian invasion of Ukraine (March 16, 2022), the article uses
the war as an empirical laboratory to taxonomize six mechanisms of digital inclusion/exclusion:

COLLUSION: instantaneous geopolitical coordination at swarm speed — sanctions enacted by
private actors before any laws were voted, unanimity reached in hours rather than days.

INCLUSION AUTHORITARIANISM: the new form of control with no concept of an outside. Unlike
exclusion authoritarianism (punctual, deals with the issue once — "the Final Solution is
explicit"), inclusion authoritarianism is relentless and never terminates. Every country
must comply; "you are going to be fined and bullied to submission." COVID nudges as prototype;
Ukraine sanctions as geopolitical version.

EXCLUSION: deplatforming at every network layer simultaneously — financial (SWIFT), social
platforms, airlines, sports bodies. Progressive disconnection rather than imprisonment;
the gradient of exclusion has no clear threshold moment.

PRECLUSION: preventive language control. Banning "war" and "invasion" from official
communications before the discourse forms. The mechanism: political talking points →
MSM adoption → social media spread. Framing as prophylaxis.

OCCLUSION: attentional monopoly of the current thing. Network winner-takes-all produces
monofocal attention that is shallow, present-tense, and incapable of mid/long-term thinking.
"Only the instant and the local problem are relevant."

SECLUSION: total network isolation as geopolitical weapon. But India and China not following
raises the question of who is secluded from whom — the assumption of a universal network
is itself a Westphalian residue.

Central thesis: the Ukraine war is a collision between two players operating under different
institutional frameworks simultaneously. Russia plays in the Westphalian (nation-state)
frame while the West has abandoned it without knowing what replaces it. "Deep misunderstanding
is what defines best our times." No "world after" exists — only ongoing transformation.
