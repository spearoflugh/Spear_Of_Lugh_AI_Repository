# Yesterday is no longer what it used to be

Photography has changed our relationship with reality. In the early times of this technology a picture was seen as a hifi recording of the past. Like a clone of reality. We quickly understood that things were more complex both from a practical point of view (as illustrated by the famous erasure of Trostky) or from a philosophical point of view (“Le réel et son double” by Clément Rosset - I don’t know if there is an english translation). Social media have boosted the creation of alternatives to reality in a way that I did not anticipated.

### Search and Destroy

[Embedded X post]

Before internet if you wanted to access yesterday you had to go through files. Physical files. You know this is this scene in the movies where the hero go down to the cellar of the local newspaper to read the news and find out the mystery around which the movie develop. Accessing the past was costly and not available to anyone. Our hero, more often than not, had to be exceptionnaly ingenious to gain access to the files. Nowdays with internet everything is stored and easily searchable. Because accessing to the files was just step 1. Then, hours of manual search were waiting for you before you could find the information you were looking for. That is, if you were lucky ! Nowadays if your answer is located on page 2 of Google search it most likely mean that you were not specific enough. Presumably this capacity to record and search the past at will was going to produce a world with less lying and more transparency…

We all know that it is the complete opposite that happened. How is it possible ? One paradoxical feature of the human memory often misunderstood is its capacity to forget. Being able to not be bothered by useless information from the past is paramount to have a clear view of your life. If you are constantly living at the level of details (the brand of the yogurt you had for breakfast 475 days ago) you will miss the big picture. But internet never forgets: it goes from political speeches to cute kiten videos on youtube passing by untold mass of porn videos… And when you don’t forget it becomes more difficult to forgive. The first idea that comes to mind is “cancel culture”.

Another idea is that humans are really masters at adaptation. Because of the sheer volume of information available, one can play the numbers and bet on human laziness. AOC faking to be arrested by puting her hands in her back is a perfect illustration of this strategy.

This is a variation on the lightning rod policy strategy.  She knows people know it is all Kabuki theater but she doesn’t care. The point is not to convince anyone but to strengthen the partisan memory of her supporters. Just like when you repeat lines of a poem that you are trying to record in your memory, or repeat moves in sports. The fact that it is obviously not linked to any kind of reality is auxiliary. This is how yesterday will be accessed tomorow on the internet. The self reflection of this process is of paramount importance.

### Self authorship

Nothing is more similar than a “1” in a computer memory cell than a “0” in the same computer memory cell. More precisely when you read “1” today you have no idea of what was written in the same place the day before. Unlike your physical memory, you can change the one stored in a computer without any side effect. This creates a strange situation in which information is no longer stable on the internet. Perhaps the best illustration of this is this wikipedia page  of a french politician that has been convicted of editing his own wikipedia page. He allegedly inserted false informations in his CV. The strangest part is that the page points to “previous versions of itself” to explain the case. Even weirder the page is using an outside source (the newspaper Le Monde) to do so! I do have to screenshot this (it is in french) because … who knows maybe tomorrow will not be like today.

Translation gives something like this:

> According to Le Monde, in an earlier version of this Wikipedia article that he wrote himself, he invented a position of "project manager" with Jean-Claude Gaudin, in 2014, and a status of "associate member". Cherpa, a research laboratory attached to the IEP of Aix-en-Provence2.
>
> On July 17, 2022, his Wikipedia account is blocked after he tried to redact the article

Self help books like “12 rules for life” are so pre social media. Nowadays you are just a few keystrokes away from self editing your public memory and create a new online version of yourself.

Who knew that transhumanism would  start internally rather than externally?
